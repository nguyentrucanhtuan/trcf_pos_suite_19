# IMC 6-Step Structure (Summary)

This summary is based on the 6-step IMC planning flow from the provided reference. Use it as the skeleton for the plan.

## Step 1: Diagnose Current Situation
- Review market context, brand position, competition, and existing channels.
- Identify the main business problem or opportunity for the campaign.

## Step 2: Define Objectives
- Translate the business problem into clear communication goals.
- Make objectives measurable and time-bound.

## Step 3: Define Target Audience and Insight
- Specify who the campaign speaks to and why they care.
- Extract a simple consumer insight that connects to the offer.

## Step 4: Big Idea and Key Message
- Create a central creative idea and a concise message that can scale across channels.
- Ensure it is relevant to the audience insight and campaign objective.

## Step 5: Channel Strategy and Implementation Plan
- Map channels, content formats, and activities to the campaign timeline.
- Allocate budget and responsibilities per channel.

## Step 6: Measurement and Optimization
- Define KPIs for awareness, engagement, and conversion.
- Outline how results will be tracked and how the plan adapts.
